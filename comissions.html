How It Works

Fill out the commission request form with your idea, preferred size, medium, and any reference photos you'd like me to use.

We'll discuss your vision together! I'll create a sketch so you can suggest changes before I begin painting.

Once the sketch is approved I'll start creating the final artwork. I'll send photo's of progress as I work!

You'll receive photos of the finished artwork. Small touch-ups can still be made before shipping.

Once final payment is complete, your artwork is carefully packaged and shipped to its new home!

pricing

commercial use

If you are planning to use your comission commercially, please refer below to understand the commercial fees.

  • minor use(e.g., website headers, limited interior use, book illustrations): 50% surcharge on base price
  • Merchandise & Advertising (e.g., local campaigns, t-shirts, album covers): 200% surcharge on base price or 5%-15% royalty
  • Mass Market & National Campaigns (e.g., large brand campaigns, items intended for wide resale): 1000% surcharge or full royalty

how i price

I charge by a base fee of $25 + the cost of materials

**IMPORTANTE NOTE!!**

size and complexity can both impact the cost of a comissions, as larger and/or more complex requests tend to take longer and can use more materials. If you are working within a certain budget, it is important to discuss it with me so we can come up with a creation that you love and can afford! if you are looking to get an approximate price calculation, you can use the price calculator below!

general prices by size

**NOTE: these prices are only general and may differ from actual comissions because of each comissions' unique requests/complexity!**

  • small pieces(up to 12"X16") usually cost $120-$395
  • medium pieces (16"X20" to 24"X24) usually cost around $400-800
  • large paintings ("24x30" and up) usually cost $1000+
