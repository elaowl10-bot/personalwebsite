I have Adhd and
Love Being Me

I have Adhd and Love Being Me is a children's book that poetically tells the story of a child with adhd, exploring the challenges, experiences, and even joys of living with the condition. It is a celebration of neurodiversity and a reminder that being different is something to be proud of.
