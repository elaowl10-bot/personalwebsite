Welcome to Creative Chaos!

We're a mother-daughter creative duo based in Rhode Island who believe that art has the power to educate, connect, and make even the most complicated topics feel a little less overwhelming.

Our journey began with a shared goal: creating resources that we wished had existed when we were learning to navigate ADHD ourselves. Together, we created I Have ADHD and I Love Being Me, a children's book inspired by our own family's experiences with ADHD. Every page reflects real emotions, challenges, victories, and lessons we've learned along the way. Our hope is that the book helps children feel understood while giving families a comforting resource to start meaningful conversations..

Meet Ela

Hi! I'm Ela, the illustrator behind our books and the artist behind much of Creative Chaos. I also designed, built, and coded this website!

I'm passionate about painting, illustration, and finding creative ways to make learning more engaging and accessible. Whether I'm creating children's books, teaching art workshops, or working on new educational projects, I love combining creativity with science, storytelling, and education to help others learn, grow, and feel inspired.

Meet Dr Sibel Algon

I'm Dr. Sibel Algon, a child psychiatrist and the author of I Have ADHD and I Love Being Me. My work has always centered around helping children and families better understand themselves and one another, and writing has become another way to extend that support beyond the therapy room.

I believe that learning should be an adventure, and I'm dedicated to creating resources that help children explore the world around them with curiosity, creativity, and confidence. Whether I'm writing, teaching, or collaborating with my daughter on new projects, I'm always looking for ways to make learning more engaging and accessible for everyone.

When I'm not working with patients or writing, you'll usually find me making whimsical handmade jewelry or dreaming up our next creative project together.
