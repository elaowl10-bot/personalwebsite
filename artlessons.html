Hi! I'm Ela🎨🤍

Ever since I knew how to hold a pencil (regardless of teachers trying to fix my weird pencil grip), I've been passionate about creating art. Art has helped me to express myself in ways words can't, and has been a constant source of joy and inspiration in my life. Art is messy just like life, but it is also beautiful and rewarding. I want to continue sharing my love for art with others through art lessons.

what I offer

I offer art lessons for all ages and skill levels. Whether you're a beginner looking to learn the basics or an experienced artist looking to refine your skills, I can help you achieve your artistic goals. Lessons can be tailored to your interests and needs, and can cover a variety of mediums including drawing, painting, and digital art.

  • i do lessons in person (for local Barrington RI students) and online via zoom for students anywhere.**
  • lessons cost $50 per hour, not including materials.
  • Please see below for information about required materials for each lesson kind.
  • if you are interested in lessons, you can fill out the lessons request form below

drawing & charcoal

You'll learn the fundamentals of drawing, including line, shape, form, and perspective. We'll also cover shading and blending techniques to bring your drawings to life.

oil painting

you'll learn the fundamentals of oil painting. Learn layering, blending, color mixing, and classic painting techniques while creating rich, expressive artwork at your own pace.

acrylic painting

Fast-drying, colorful, and full of possibilities! Acrylics are perfect for experimenting with texture, bold brushstrokes, and vibrant paintings on canvas. You'll learn techniques that can be adapted to almost any style.

gouache

Opaque, vibrant, and incredibly versatile! Learn how to create smooth blends, bold colors, and beautiful illustrations using one of my favorite mediums. Great for beginners and experienced artists alike.
